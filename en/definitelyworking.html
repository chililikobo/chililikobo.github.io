Product Overview

"Give the illusion that you are engaged in deep, critical work."

DefinitelyWorking is a full-screen joke application that transforms your display into high-tech consoles—such as a cyberpunk hacker terminal, an AI research cluster, or a high-frequency trading (Quant) workspace. Packed with dynamic animations and hilarious developer memes, it instantly puts your workstation into "ultra-busy mode" whenever you want to deter prying eyes or take a discreet break.

5 High-Tech Themes

Switch instantly between 5 presets (Hacker, Quant Trading, AI Training, Server Monitoring, Quantum Core) using the F1–F5 keys.

Dynamic Humor Logs

Real-time updating console logs like "Downloading more RAM" and "Encrypting boss's coffee machine" bring continuous tech humor.

Stealth UI Switching

A discreet stealth dropdown menu emerges only when hovering your cursor over the bottom-right corner, keeping the interface subtle.

Native AOT Performance

03

Press [Alt] + [F4] or click the close button at any time to return to your normal desktop.
