Product Overview

"From 256px to 16px. Relentlessly engineered for micro-dot clarity."

PinSharp ICO is a specialized Windows icon utility designed to solve common downscaling problems: white borders turning muddy gray, dark narrow slits vanishing, and sharp contours blurring away into vague shapes at small sizes like 16×16 or 24×24.

Downscaling a 256×256px original image down to 16×16px represents an extreme 1/256 area reduction. PinSharp ICO preserves high contrast for delicate borders, typography, and narrow gaps, generating multi-resolution Windows ICO files that stand out crisply on taskbars, title bars, and File Explorer.

Extreme Downscaling Clarity

Retains high contrast and vibrant color intensity for delicate outlines and slits even at micro-pixel dimensions.

Drag-and-Drop Auto Saving

Simply drop your 256×256 PNG. Conversion starts instantly, saving the finished `.ico` alongside your source image.

All 11 Standard Windows Sizes

Full coverage for 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 72, 96, 128, and 256px. Presets (All / Standard / Compact) ready.

Zero-Halo Boundary Clamping

Mathematical boundary clamping fundamentally eliminates unnatural white fringe and halo artifacts around edges.

Zero Installation & Native AOT Execution

PinSharp ICO is compiled with Native AOT technology. It requires absolutely no .NET runtime or external framework installations.

Simply extract the folder and double-click `PinSharpICO.exe` to run immediately on any PC. It leaves zero registry footprints and runs directly from a USB flash drive. To uninstall, simply delete the folder.
