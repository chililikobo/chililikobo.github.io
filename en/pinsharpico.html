PinSharp ICO is compiled with Native AOT technology, making it completely standalone and easy to carry on a USB flash drive.

It requires absolutely no additional runtimes like .NET. Simply extract the folder containing `PinSharpICO.exe` alongside its accompanying DLLs (`Magick.Native-Q8-x64.dll`, `libSkiaSharp.dll`, `libHarfBuzzSharp.dll`, and `av_libglesv2.dll`), and run it directly on any PC. It leaves zero registry footprints—uninstall anytime simply by deleting the folder.

Product Overview

"From 256px to 16px. Relentlessly engineered for micro-dot clarity."

Standard downscaling tools frequently introduce frustrating artifacts when generating icons: white outlines turn into muddy gray, tight dark slits vanish, and edges blur out into vague silhouettes at 16×16 or 24×24.

PinSharp ICO tackles this extreme compression process (a 1/256 area reduction) through a dedicated perceptual downscaling engine. It preserves sharp contrast for fine borders, typography, and narrow gaps, generating multi-resolution Windows `.ico` files that stand out crisply on taskbars, title bars, and notifications.

Key Features

  • Engineered for Extreme 256px → 16px Downscaling: Retains high contrast and vibrant color intensity for delicate outlines and slits even when compressed down to micro-pixel dimensions.
  • Automated Instant Saving on Drag-and-Drop: Simply drop your 256×256 PNG file into the window. Processing starts instantly, saving the finished `.ico` file into the same directory without prompting save dialogs.
  • Complete Coverage of All 11 Standard Windows Sizes: Fully supports 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 72, 96, 128, and 256 px. Quickly switch configurations using presets (All, Standard, Compact) or individual buttons.
  • Zero-Halo Boundary Clamping: Employs mathematical local bounding box clamping to fundamentally eliminate unnatural white halos and ringing artifacts around high-contrast contours.
  • Ascending Size Previews: Instantly displays up to 5 result preview thumbnails in ascending order (smallest first) upon completion, allowing immediate legibility checks.
