Pro Trial (Unregistered) vs. Pro License — 3 Reasons to Upgrade

1. Client-Ready & Audit-Ready Licensed Attribution

Reports generated with the unregistered trial (HTML / MD / CSV) display a Trial (Unregistered) stamp. A Pro License replaces it with your registered license attribution & project label (e.g., Licensed to: ... | Project: ...), making every report ready to hand directly to clients or file as audit evidence.

2. Continuous Multi-PC Scanning from One USB or Network Share

Because the unregistered trial automatically deletes ohnuki-pro.dat after a single run, it cannot be reused across machines. A Pro License lets you turn off auto-deletion so you can scan any number of PCs back-to-back from one USB drive or shared folder (while keeping optional post-scan cleanup when needed).

3. Up to 30-Day Expiry for Advance Prep & Remote ZIP Distribution

Unregistered trials are locked to 24 hours, which easily expires if you prepare scanners the day before or email ZIP packages to remote staff. A Pro License lets you choose 1, 3, 7, 14, or 30 days—plenty of time for advance kitting prep and remote collection (safely reverting to Standard Free mode once expired).

Host license.dat uses machine-bound AES-256-GCM encryption with a 30-day offline grace period. Field ohnuki-pro.dat supports clock rollback protection and optional post-scan self-deletion.
